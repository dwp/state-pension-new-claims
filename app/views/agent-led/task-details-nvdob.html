Unverified date of birth (UDOB)

You can verify the date of birth if the evidence provided matches the date we have in the claimant's record.

You will need to:

  • send the claimant an MF5 or MF4 requesting a birth certificate or secondary evidence
  • check ECMS for evidence
  • verify the claimant's date of birth
  • record anything you have done with 'add an action'
