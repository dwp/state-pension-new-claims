Non verified date of birth (NVDOB)

In this task you will need to match the claimants date of birth in GySP with the evidence they have provided.

You will need to:

  • send the claimant an MF5 or MF4 requesting a birth certificate or secondary evidence
  • check ECMS for evidence
  • verify the claimant's date of birth
  • record your actions
