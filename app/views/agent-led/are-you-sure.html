Closing the claim

Before proceeding, you need to have sent a letter to the claimant telling them we have closed their claim. They will need to claim again to get their State Pension.

Only close the claim if:

  • we have already requested information 3 times without success
  • we have received insufficient or conflicting information
  • the claimant does not meet the minimum qualifications for State Pension
  • the claimant does not want their state pension
